# Story Bank -- Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any interview question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file -- your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" -- combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" -- pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" -- find a story with a Reflection

### [Governance] BMC PMO Standards Build
**Source:** Report #001 -- J&J MedTech -- Director of Project Management
**S (Situation):** BMC had no standardized PM methodology across 4 portfolios and newly acquired hospitals creating fragmented governance.
**T (Task):** Build a coherent PMO governance model for a 150-person PM org with 50+ concurrent projects.
**A (Action):** Led PMO to establish project standards, methodologies, and best practices; redesigned demand/prioritization process for fast-growing health system.
**R (Result):** Unified governance model adopted enterprise-wide; improved forecasting accuracy for $160M portfolio; standardized exec reporting across CapEx/OpEx.
**Reflection:** Would instrument governance adoption metrics from day one -- not just build the framework, measure whether teams are actually using it. Frameworks without adoption tracking are shelf-ware.
**Best for questions about:** governance frameworks, PMO buildout, standards and process, portfolio management, enterprise PM leadership

### [Financial] BMC $160M Portfolio Oversight
**Source:** Report #001 -- J&J MedTech -- Director of Project Management
**S (Situation):** BMC's IT portfolio ($150M CapEx, $10-12M OpEx) had fragmented executive visibility across projects with varying levels of budget discipline.
**T (Task):** Maintain exec-level visibility into forecasts, burn rates, and variances for a complex mixed-project portfolio.
**A (Action):** Built reporting cadences, worked with finance to align capital forecasts, surfaced variance flags to Associate CIO through structured governance forums.
**R (Result):** Sustained fiscal accountability through FY with no material variance surprises; earned role as strategic advisor to Associate CIO and VP of Digital Strategy.
**Reflection:** Would push for real-time dashboards earlier -- static reporting creates dangerous lag in complex portfolios when projects shift mid-quarter.
**Best for questions about:** budget management, financial oversight, portfolio governance, executive communication, C-suite advisory

### [Process] BMC Demand Process Redesign
**Source:** Report #001 -- J&J MedTech -- Director of Project Management
**S (Situation):** BMC's project demand intake was designed for a smaller health system and not keeping pace with acquisitions of community hospitals and clinics.
**T (Task):** Redesign demand and prioritization process to serve a larger, more complex and geographically distributed organization.
**A (Action):** Mapped current state, identified friction points, rebuilt intake with weighted scoring aligned to strategic goals, co-designed with PM leads and business sponsors.
**R (Result):** Process adopted organization-wide; improved turnaround for intake decisions; reduced backlog of unscored/unstaged work; provided better strategic alignment signal for leadership.
**Reflection:** Involve end-users in the design phase every time. Every time I've skipped co-design, I've had to redo it. The best process improvements feel like they were the team's idea -- because they were.
**Best for questions about:** process improvement, standardization, PMO redesign, stakeholder co-design, organizational scalability

### [Governance] AI Governance in Regulated Environments
**Source:** Report #001 -- J&J MedTech -- Director of Project Management
**S (Situation):** BMC stood up an AI Governance Committee as AI adoption accelerated across clinical and operational functions.
**T (Task):** Represent IT Portfolio function on committee; evaluate AI projects for strategic alignment and risk.
**A (Action):** Assessed AI proposals against strategic, clinical, and technical criteria; provided PM-lens risk framing (delivery risk, resource, timeline, integration complexity) alongside clinical and ethical review.

### [Internal Transition] Applying Company Context to New Domain
**Source:** Report #009 -- Takeda AD PM Lead, Global Oncology BU
**S (Situation):** After Takeda restructured and eliminated the DD&T digital innovation role, needed to pivot PM expertise into a new functional area while leveraging existing company knowledge.
**T (Task):** Demonstrate transferability of program management discipline from digital/AI to commercial oncology operations.
**A (Action):** Mapped TakVisor operating model (cross-BU governance, financial oversight, stakeholder alignment, risk escalation) to GBT/IBT brand team structure. Identified structural parallels: both require cross-functional matrix leadership without direct authority.
**R (Result):** Clear articulation of transferable skills with specific proof points for every JD requirement. Internal candidacy provides culture/systems knowledge that external hires lack.
**Reflection:** Domain expertise is learnable; program management discipline is not. The PM operating system transfers across functions — the vocabulary changes, the rigor stays the same.
**Best for questions about:** career transitions, transferable skills, internal mobility, domain learning, adaptability
